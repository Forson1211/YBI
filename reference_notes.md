# Reference Direction: World Inspiring Network

The provided reference uses a white, compact header with the organization logo on the left, a simple navigation menu, and a high-contrast donate button on the right. On mobile, the header becomes a very short horizontal bar with a hamburger menu and a support/donation icon, followed by the hero image immediately below.

The hero is the dominant first impression: a full-height documentary photograph with a dark translucent overlay, large bold white headline, centered or left-centered copy depending on breakpoint, and a simple white support CTA. The visual tone is direct, optimistic, community-centered, and image-first rather than editorial or decorative.

The homepage sequence is a nonprofit storytelling funnel: hero, organization introduction, problem statements, solution/focus areas, initiatives/program cards, reasons to trust the organization, volunteer or participation CTA, news and articles, newsletter, and a dark footer with navigation, donation CTA, and social links.

For Young Beginners Inspiration, the updated implementation will retain that sequence and interaction pattern while replacing the reference greens with the supplied logo system: deep YBI blue for header/footer and overlays, red for emphasis, yellow for support actions, orange for secondary accents, and white/cream for content surfaces. All copy remains specific to leadership, education, business, public speaking, and entrepreneurship.
